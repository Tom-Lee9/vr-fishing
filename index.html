🎣 VR Fishing: Noosa River

Turn your phone sideways. Tap Start, allow motion access, then slide it into the headset.

Hold the screen (or headset button) to charge a cast, let go to throw it where you're looking. When the bobber gets pulled under, tap fast to hook the fish. Then hold to reel in, and let go when the bar goes red or the line snaps. Hold with no fish on to wind back in. Tap to close the catch screen. Your catch log is on your left. Two-finger tap re-centres.

Joy-Cons: hold ZR or R to cast, press it to hook, spin the right stick in circles to reel, + re-centres.
