🎣 VR Fishing: Noosa River

Turn your phone sideways. Tap Start, allow motion access, then slide it into the headset.

Hold the screen (or headset button) to charge a cast, let go to throw it where you're looking. When the bobber gets pulled under, tap fast to hook the fish. Then hold to reel in. When the fish makes a run (RUN!), stop winding and let it take line, or the line snaps. The white mark on the bar is your drag; the number shows how much line is left. Hold with no fish on to wind back in. After a catch, look at Keep or Release and tap. Look at the Aquarium button under your catch log to visit your fish. Your catch log is on your left. Two-finger tap re-centres.

Joy-Cons: hold ZR or R to cast, press it to hook, spin the right stick in circles to reel, + re-centres.

Buy a boat in the Aquarium's Shop, then look at "Take the boat" under the catch log. Push a stick to drive. Look down-left at the dashboard to get back on the jetty.
