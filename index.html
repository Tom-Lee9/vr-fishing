🎣 VR Fishing

Turn your phone sideways. Tap Start, allow motion access, then slide it into the headset.

Hold the screen (or headset button) to charge a cast, let go to throw it where you're looking. When the bobber gets pulled under, tap fast to hook the fish. Then hold to reel in, and let go when the bar goes red or the line snaps. Hold with no fish on to wind back in. Two-finger tap re-centres.
